## Recommended Image Specifications

- **Format**: JPEG (.jpg) or WebP for better compression
- **Size**: 1200x600px minimum (hero images)
- **Quality**: High quality, 72-150 KB per image (optimized)
- **Style**: Professional travel photography, high-resolution

## Recommended Websites for Free Images

1. **Unsplash** (https://unsplash.com)
   - High-quality, free stock photos
   - CC0 license (no attribution required)
   - Excellent for travel destinations

2. **Pexels** (https://www.pexels.com)
   - Free stock photography
   - CC0 license
   - Good selection of South Africa travel photos

3. **Pixabay** (https://pixabay.com)
   - Free images and videos
   - CC0 license
   - Large collection of travel destinations

## How to Add Images

1. Download images from the sources above
2. Rename them to match the filenames above
3. Place them in: `frontend/public/images/destinations/`
4. Ensure images are optimized for web (compressed)

## Notes

- All image paths in the component are already configured
- The component will display a hero image for each destination
- Images will be displayed in a responsive container
- Optimize images before adding to reduce bundle size
